## 0. Mission (read this first)

> **Broker OS runs the practice end to end. You stay with the human.**

Broker OS manages listing inventory, runs social campaigns, optimizes marketing spend, and holds
the calendar, so independents can compete with top firms without becoming software operators.
Every section on the page traces back to this. Cut copy that does not.

## 1. Product Overview

- **Name:** Broker OS  
- **Category line:** The Agentic OS for real estate consultants.  
- **What it is:** Firm-grade OS for independent practices. End-to-end ops so you focus on people.  
- **Four pillars:** Inventory → Campaigns → Spend → Calendar  
- **One-liner:** Superpowers for independents. Focus on the human.  
- **Type:** B2B SaaS waitlist / early access. Proof in `ads-agent/`.

## 2. Audience

**Real Estate Consultants** — independent CRE and residential. Solo or small practice. Competing
with larger firms for the same mandates and buyers.

## 3. Problems

**Headline:** Your practice runs on broken, siloed workflows.  
Listings never share one source of truth. Campaigns start over for every listing. Spend cannot
explain the enquiry. The calendar fills before clients get you.

## 4. Differentiation

Entire practice on one OS. Inventory and campaigns, spend and calendar. Human gate on spend and
sends. Leveling claim: compete with top firms while staying with the human work.

## 5. Language

**Use:** Broker OS, Agentic OS, practice, desk, inventory, listings, social campaigns, marketing
spend, calendar, human, independents, top firms / big firms, approve, drafts, early access.

**Avoid:** manifesto tone, revolutionize, seamless, next-gen, unlock, elevate, "AI-powered" as
adjective, unsupervised autopilot / "AI runs your ads", competitor brand names, landlord property
management, bare three-item comma lists (pair pillars instead).

## 7. How it works

1. **Inventory** — Put the listing on the desk. Broker OS keeps the brief and status with it.  
2. **Campaigns and spend** — Agents draft social campaigns. You approve spend. Performance shows what worked.  
3. **Calendar** — Outreach and tours queue on your calendar. You approve sends. People get your best hours.

## 8. Agents block

> Inventory and campaigns, spend and calendar, share one desk and one approve gate.

Headline: **Agents that run the practice with you.**

| Calendar | Tours and follow-ups land where they belong. Space opens for the human work. |
