* **Date:** 2026-10-30
* **Method:** Firecrawl searches and page scrapes. Each claim previously marked "not checked", "not verified" or "a reviewer said" in [concepts_brief.md](concepts_brief.md) or [dual_market_rollout.md](dual_market_rollout.md) is listed with what the sources say.
* **Status values:**

  * **Supported:** secondary sources only.
  * **Corrected:** the earlier claim was wrong, and this entry replaces it.
  * **Open:** no source found.
