### Mission (read this first)

> **Broker OS runs the practice end to end. You stay with the human.**

Broker OS is the Agentic OS for real estate consultants. It manages **listing inventory**, runs
**social campaigns**, **optimizes marketing spend**, and **holds the calendar**, so independents can
**compete with top firms** without becoming software operators. The consultant's time goes to people.
Every section on the page exists to prove this. If new copy does not trace back to it, cut the copy.

**Inventory means listings and mandates** (what the consultant is marketing). It does **not** mean
landlord property management (still out of scope).

## 5. The four pillars (mission architecture)

Every section on the page maps to one of these four. Problem pain points, solution pillars, agent
cards, and how-it-works steps all use this same order and these same names, not synonyms.

| # | Pillar | Job | On-page name |
|---|--------|-----|---------------|
| 1 | **Inventory** | Keep listing / mandate inventory current on one desk | Inventory |
| 2 | **Campaigns** | Social/paid campaign management that generates demand from a listing | Campaigns |
| 3 | **Spend** | Optimize marketing spend; see what earned enquiries vs what wasted budget | Spend |
| 4 | **Calendar** | Hold tours and follow-ups so people get the consultant's best hours | Calendar |

How-it-works collapses into three steps for scanability: **Inventory** → **Campaigns and spend** → **Calendar**.
Spend stays a named pillar in problem/solution cards even when paired with campaigns in step 02.

### Traditional tools vs Broker OS

| Traditional stack | Broker OS |
|-------------------|-----------|
| Folders, chats, ad accounts, calendar apps | One desk for the whole practice |
| Manual campaign rebuild per listing | Agents draft; you approve spend |
| Spend with no attribution | Spend signal next to the enquiry |
| Day owned by the calendar | Calendar cleared for human work |
