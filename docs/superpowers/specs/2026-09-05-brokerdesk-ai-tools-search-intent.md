## Verdict

Brokers (mostly residential-coded, CRE-light) **do search for AI** — but they search for **AI tools / AI agents for real estate**, not “CRE AI” and not “Broker OS.”

| Cluster | What they type | Vol signal | Implication for BrokerDesk |
|---------|----------------|------------|----------------------------|
| **Category discovery** | ai for real estate, real estate ai, ai for real estate agents | 880–2,400 | Primary SEO / ad / waitlist category language |
| **Agent product** | ai agents for real estate, ai agent for real estate, real estate ai agent | 390–720 | **Best category match** for our product story |
| **Tool shopping** | ai tools for real estate, real estate ai tools, best ai for real estate agents | 170–720 | They expect **listicles of point tools** — we must differentiate |
| **DIY LLM** | chatgpt for real estate / realtors / agents | 210–260 | Competing habit: prompt pile, not a desk |
| **Point-tool gravity** | virtual staging ai **2,400**; content AI brands; phone AI; chatbots | High | SERP is full of staging/content/voice widgets |
| **CRE AI** | ai for commercial real estate, ai for cre | **30–90** | Do **not** lead SEO on “CRE AI”; use CRE in proof + CRM/marketing language |
| **Fear / hype** | will ai replace real estate agents (260); ai real estate agent (720) | Medium | Must say: **you decide and close**; agent runs grind |

**Positioning pivot:** Enter as the **AI agent for independent brokers** (search language), then prove you’re **not another AI tool** — you run marketing, enquiries, and follow-up as one desk (pain + CRE CRM jobs).

## 3. Messaging house (AI-tools-aligned v6)

### Category line (search-native)

> The AI agent for independent real estate brokers.

### Differentiation line (vs AI tools SERP)

> Not another AI tool. One agent that runs your desk.

### Pain → promise (still required for conversion)

> Portal noise, expensive ads, and CRMs that rot. BrokerDesk is the AI agent that runs demand, follow-up, and pipeline — CRE and residential — while you decide and close.

### One-liner

> BrokerDesk is the AI agent for independent CRE and residential brokers — it runs marketing, catches every enquiry, and keeps deals moving, so you stop stacking AI tools and start closing.

### Punch

> The agent runs the desk. You decide and close.

### Primary headlines (AI entry + desk clarity)

| ID | Headline | Why |
|----|----------|-----|
| **A1 ★** | `The AI agent for your whole broker desk — not another AI tool.` | Matches “AI agent” search + fights tool-list SERP |
| **A2** | `Stop collecting AI tools. Run one agent.` | Direct anti-listicle |
| **A3** | `AI for real estate agents that actually runs the desk.` | Mirrors high-vol “AI for real estate agents” |
| **A4** | `ChatGPT isn’t a brokerage. This is.` | Against DIY LLM habit |

### Recommended waitlist copy

- **Eyebrow:** `AI AGENT · CRE & RESIDENTIAL`  
- **Headline:** `The AI agent for your whole broker desk — not another AI tool.`  
- **Sub:** `Staging apps, chatbots, and ChatGPT tabs don’t run your practice. BrokerDesk attracts demand, captures every enquiry, and keeps follow-up moving — commercial and residential — while you approve spend and close.`  
- **CTA:** `Join waitlist`  
- **Micro:** `Human in command. Built for independents — not franchises, not AI that replaces you.`  

### Shorter sub

> One AI agent for marketing, enquiries, and deal follow-through — CRE and residential. Not a pile of AI widgets.

### PAS (AI-aware)

- **Problem:** You’re collecting AI tools and still losing leads in WhatsApp and a CRM you never update.  
- **Agitate:** Staging AI, ChatGPT, and chatbots don’t run campaigns, catch inbounds, or stop CRM rot. CRE and residential still feel like two jobs.  
- **Solution:** One AI agent for the desk — demand, capture, follow-through — you decide and close.

### Outcomes strip

1. **One agent, not twelve tools**  
2. **Demand you control** (human-gated ads)  
3. **Every enquiry caught**  
4. **CRM that doesn’t rot**  

### FAQ

1. **Is this another AI writing / staging tool?** → No. Those are point tools. This agent runs marketing, enquiries, and pipeline.  
2. **Will AI replace me?** → No. You approve spend and close. The agent owns the grind.  
3. **I already use ChatGPT** → ChatGPT is a tab. BrokerDesk is the desk.  
4. **CRE and residential?** → Same agent. Same message.  

### SEO / content targets (post-waitlist)

Prioritize pages/ads around: `ai agent for real estate`, `ai agents for real estate`, `ai for real estate agents` — with clear “not a tool list” angle.  
Do **not** over-invest in `ai for commercial real estate` volume; use CRE in body + `commercial real estate crm` / marketing clusters from prior pass.
