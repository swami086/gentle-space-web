# Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-07-30*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Bangalore commercial real estate consulting firm for custom leasing requirements.
**What it does:** Matches companies to commercial space — office, retail, warehouse/industrial, or other commercial property types — on location, budget, size, building quality, commute, and lease terms. For property owners, sources screened tenants and helps lease on clear, high-trust terms.
**Product category:** CRE consulting / brokerage (not a self-serve listings portal).
**Product type:** Service, with a secondary self-serve product line.
**Business model:** No fee for standard requirements within existing inventory. Fixed fee, agreed upfront, for highly customised searches outside that inventory.

**Secondary product line:** `/spaces` — a self-serve coworking/managed-office listings portal, part of the same business but a lighter-touch browse experience. The homepage sells the consulting relationship; `/spaces` is linked out to, not sold directly on the homepage.

**Important positioning correction:** Copy must reflect that Gentle Space specialises in **all types of commercial real estate** — not just office and coworking. Current copy over-indexes on office/coworking language; the rewrite should broaden this (office, retail, warehouse/industrial, F&B/hospitality, etc.).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Spans three sub-segments, spoken to equally (no single priority segment):
- Growing startups (Series B+ SaaS, etc.)
- GCCs / MNC India operations entering Bangalore
- Established local companies (e.g. insurance, healthcare)

Plus a separate, equally important audience: **property owners** seeking tenants.

**Decision-makers:** A mix — Admin/Ops heads, Founders/CEOs, and India Ops heads for GCCs. Copy should not assume a single title.
**Primary use case:** Roughly equal split between first office in Bangalore (new to market) and expansion/relocation (existing footprint growing).
**Jobs to be done:**
- Find commercial space that fits a specific, non-generic brief
- Close a lease without legal/commercial risk exposure
- (Property owners) Fill vacant space with a reliable tenant at fair market rent

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem (companies):** Property listings are inflated and hide legal/commercial risk. Generic portals and brokers surface volume, not fit — leading to wasted tours on irrelevant floors.
**Core problem (property owners):** Finding a reliable, screened tenant without sitting on vacant space or guessing at rent.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Self-serve listing portals: no verification, no legal/paperwork support, no custom-brief matching
- Big brokerages: built for large enterprise mandates, slower and less personal for mid-size or first-time-in-Bangalore companies
- DIY (internal facilities/ops team): no market data, no negotiation leverage, full legal burden falls on them
**What it costs them:** Weeks lost touring dead-end options; legal/verification risk if unmanaged; below-market rent or long vacancy (owners).
**Emotional tension:** Distrust of inflated listings; anxiety about paperwork/compliance surprises; owners worry about tenant reliability.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Brief-led matching, not generic inventory
- End-to-end verification, legal, and paperwork support through handover, plus renewals and expansions
- No fee for standard requirements
- Sees both sides of the market (tenants + owners), sharpening rent/positioning data
- Covers all commercial real estate types, not just office
**How we do it differently:** Start from the custom brief, pre-vet and verify before it reaches the shortlist, then stay through legal close and renewals — one relationship, not five vendors.
**Why that's better:** Less legal/commercial risk, faster to a signed lease, fair market pricing instead of guesswork.
**Why customers choose us:** High-trust, personal process versus an impersonal big brokerage or an unsupported self-serve listings site. Verification/legal/end-to-end support is the primary driver; the no-fee model is a strong secondary reason, not the headline reason.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push (away from current):** Inflated listings, wasted tours on irrelevant floors, legal/paperwork risk exposure, unreliable local brokers.
**Pull (toward us):** Verified custom shortlist, no fee for standard requirements, end-to-end legal/paperwork support, fast turnaround.
**Habit (keeping them stuck):** Existing broker relationship; "just ask around" or DIY via internal facilities/ops team.
**Anxiety (about switching):** Will they actually understand our specific brief; are they pushing their own inventory; is "no fee" too good to be true / hidden costs later.

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** Professional but approachable — contractions okay, no jargon.
**Style:** Direct, plain, matter-of-fact.
**Personality:** Understated, measured, calm, confident without hype. 🔴 *Assumed — not explicitly confirmed; revisit if drafts read wrong.*
**Voice DO's:** Lead with customer outcome, not company name. Use specific numbers/timeframes where real. Use direct address ("you").
**Voice DON'T's:** No exclamation points. No unverified stats or invented testimonials. No keyword-stuffed repetition ("custom requirements" every sentence).

## Style Guide

**Preferred terms:** "Bangalore" (not "Bengaluru"); "commercial real estate" broadly, not "office space" as a synonym for the whole business.
